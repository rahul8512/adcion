01 / ABOUT ADCOIN

A TOKEN BUILT
ON CLARITY.

ADCOIN (ADCN) is a digital cryptocurrency token built on the BNB Smart Chain (BSC) using the BEP-20 token standard.

ADCOIN aims to establish a modern digital token ecosystem focused on transparency, accessibility, and blockchain technology. By utilizing the BNB Smart Chain, ADCN benefits from an established blockchain infrastructure designed for efficient and cost-effective transactions.

With a fixed total supply of 50 million ADCN tokens and minting disabled, ADCOIN is designed around a predefined token supply.
